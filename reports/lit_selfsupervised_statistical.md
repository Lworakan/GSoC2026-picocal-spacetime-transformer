## Notes

1. Identifiability flag (asked explicitly): yes, there is theory that event-level labels can suffice. Zhang et al. (NeurIPS 2020, #10) prove learnability/consistency for instance-level regression when only sums over bags are labeled, provided bag composition varies; Scott & Zhang (NeurIPS 2020, #11) give the identifiability conditions for the proportion/classification version; CWoLa (#9) is the HEP-native precedent. Interpretation for us: the free-learned gate already sits in this framework - the theory says the leverage comes from diversity of window compositions, so training the gate jointly on clean AND min-bias windows (two bags with very different pileup proportions) with an explicit aggregate likelihood is theoretically grounded, not a hack.

2. Cheapest untried wins with existing supervision: quantile/heteroscedastic heads (#4, #5). CMS got 12-15% effective-width improvement from adding resolution estimation to the same event-level labels we have. Low risk, direct sigma_eff relevance, per-bin calibration falls out for free.

3. The recommendation to bring to the mentor: real zero-bias overlay (#12). Every falsified approach failed for one of two reasons - no per-cell truth, or synthetic pileup domain gap. Embedding real random-trigger PicoCal events onto simulated single photons fixes both simultaneously and is standard collaboration practice (ATLAS data overlay, STAR/ALICE embedding). It requires only a zero-bias stream and digitization-level additivity, both of which a calorimeter group can provide cheaply. It would also unlock #1 (two real overlays of one photon = Noise2Noise pair) and #17 (overlay pairs = contrastive positives).

4. Self-supervised pretraining (#13-16) is the publishable 2024-2026 axis: masked-cell modeling on the unlabeled min-bias files, fine-tune on event-level energy. Orthogonal to #12 and runnable today with zero new data.

5. Charged self-labeling (#8) is the one genuinely label-free supervision source in real data: if LHCb track extrapolation to PicoCal cells is accessible, charged-matched cells carry free vertex-based signal/pileup labels that transfer to photon cells. Worth one question to the mentor.

6. OmniFold (#6) does not solve our problem: unfolding sharpens distributions, not per-event predictions; sigma_eff is a per-event quantity.
