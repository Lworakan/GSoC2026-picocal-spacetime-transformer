# Per-cell gate supervision: the oracle headroom is not reachable this way

2026-08-16. RTX 5090, 2 seeds per arm — **screening only, not quotable**. Champion baseline
`SubNetW4CleanAuxExDnQdEma` scored on the same two seeds so the comparison is like-for-like.

## Why three arms and not one

`--gatesup` turns on two things at once: the per-cell gate loss *and* ~9.7k synthetic overlay
events added to training. A single run could not tell which caused any change, which is the
entire question. `--synaux` was added as the control: overlay events, no gate loss.

- **A** champion, no overlay
- **B** `--synaux` — overlay as data only → B−A is the data effect
- **C** `--gatesup 1.0` / `5.0` — overlay plus the gate loss → C−B is the supervision effect

## Result (delta vs champion, negative is better)

| bin | err | B data only | C1 gs=1.0 | C2 gs=5.0 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| all / all | 0.0004 | +0.0005 | **−0.0007** | **−0.0010** |
| 15mm low | 0.0072 | **+0.0250** | +0.0042 | +0.0202 |
| 30mm low | 0.0033 | −0.0006 | **−0.0056** | −0.0045 |
| 40mm low | 0.0018 | +0.0018 | −0.0022 | −0.0011 |
| 60mm low | 0.0014 | −0.0004 | −0.0007 | −0.0010 |

## What it means

**The synthetic overlay data hurts on its own.** Arm B is worse than the champion everywhere it
moves at all, and badly so at 15mm low-E (+0.0250, over 3x its error). The transplanted pileup
evidently does not match the real min-bias distribution closely enough to train on directly,
despite passing the density and seed-fraction checks in `make_overlay.py`.

**The gate loss repairs most of that damage but adds little beyond it.** C1−B is −0.0012
aggregate and −0.0050 at 30mm low-E; but C1 versus the champion is −0.0007 aggregate, under
2x its error, and 30mm low-E −0.0056 at 1.7x error. On 2 seeds neither survives as a claim.

**The oracle-gate headroom does not transfer.** The paired sample showed an oracle gate reaching
0.1046 against 0.1961 for the learned gate at 30mm low-E, a 1.9x gap that motivated this run.
Supervising the gate directly against that same per-cell truth moves the real-sample metric by
at most 0.005. Knowing the target for the gate is not the same as being able to reach it from
the inputs available — the gap is not a modelling failure the loss function can address.

**15mm low-E is untouched.** It sits at 0.15-0.17 in every arm, against the 0.07 goal. Nothing
in this experiment moves it, and the arm that adds data makes it worse.

## Verified along the way

- `MATMUL_OK` on RTX 5090: torch 2.7/cu128 has working sm_120 kernels.
- Test split stays pure: `splits_for(keep, len(main_events))` indexes main events only, so
  overlay events enter through `ctr` and can never land in the scored split.

## Next

Do not spend more GPU on gate supervision. The claim in the project docs that the emergent gate
correlates r = 0.92 with truth still needs correcting against the per-cell measurement of 0.211
before anything is published.
