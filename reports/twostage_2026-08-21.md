# SpaceTformer two-stage: point, re-cut, measure — first architecture that beats the champion

2026-08-21 evening. Continues reports/night_run_2026-08-21.md. Fixed split, final base
configuration (W8 + Ex + Dn, qd, EMA) throughout.

## The mechanism

Stage 1 (**pointer**): the trained --aux members predict the photon's offset from the seed,
in cells, from the window contents alone. Stage 2 (**measurer**): the window is re-cut around
the pointed position (`--rc-mode pred`, scripts/gen_pointer.py + picocal_data rc branch) and
the energy regressor is trained on the re-pointed windows. Lineage: Spatial Transformer
Networks and Deformable DETR — learn where to look before measuring — applied to calorimetry,
where every production reconstruction uses a fixed seed- or barycentre-centred window.

## Why this was the right lever (all measured today)

- Oracle-centre (true photon position as window centre): 0.0383 single member vs the champion
  member mean 0.0399 — perfect pointing is worth -4% aggregate, -10% weak bins.
- The pointer is essentially perfect at the cell scale: **median error 0.164 cells,
  p90 0.389 cells** over the full sample — against the seed's 0.43 median with an 8.5-cell
  p90 tail at 15mm, and the barycentre's 3.08-cell median drag. No wrong-photon tail.
- A frame subtlety cost one wrong turn and is worth recording: the aux target ya is
  ALREADY seed-relative in cells ((ax-xs)/ps). Converted with the wrong (absolute-coordinate)
  scale it looked like a 9-cell pointer; in the correct frame it is a 0.16-cell pointer. The
  intermediate conclusion "aux gain is representation shaping, not localisation" was wrong
  and is retracted: the aux head genuinely localises.

## Result

| member seeds | values | mean |
|---|---|---|
| champion (Rc) | 5 seeds | 0.0399 +- 0.0005 |
| + aux | 10 seeds | 0.0395 |
| oracle centre (bound) | 1 seed | 0.0383 |
| **two-stage (Rp)** | 0.0389 / 0.0392 / 0.0386 | **0.0389 +- 0.0003** |

The two-stage members capture ~60% of the champion-to-oracle gap and are the three best
single members of the project. The 2-seed mini-ensemble already reads 0.0385 aggregate with
**30mm low-E 0.0675** — better than the champion 5-seed ensemble's 0.0717 and within reach of
the oracle's 0.0687 single — confirming the gain concentrates exactly where the centring
error analysis said it would.

Honesty note: stage-2 training windows use pointer predictions on events the pointer saw in
training; the reported test numbers use held-out pointer predictions (aux models never saw
the test split). An end-to-end version would remove the two-pass plumbing.

## Final numbers (2026-08-22, 5 seeds complete)

| ensemble (fixed split) | aggregate | paired vs champion |
|---|---|---|
| champion (Rc, 5 seeds) | 0.0388 | — |
| aux (10 seeds) | 0.0380 | -0.0008 +- 0.0003, P 1.00 |
| **two-stage Rp (5 seeds)** | **0.0378** | **-0.0010 +- 0.0003, P 1.00** |

Rp vs aux10: -0.0003 +- 0.0004, P 0.73 — Rp edges aux with half the members, but the two are
not yet separated; they are complementary claims anyway (aux = cheap one-pass, Rp = the
architecture).

Per-region low-E tercile, Rp vs champion: 15mm 0.0670 vs 0.0711 (-6%), 30mm 0.0675 vs
0.0717 (-6%), 40mm 0.0598 vs 0.0610, 60mm 0.0512 vs 0.0524, but **120mm 0.0581 vs 0.0555
(worse)** — consistent with the old rc-regions finding that the outermost region prefers the
seed centre (occupancy is low, the seed is already the photon, and re-pointing only adds
noise). Refinement available: apply the pointer in regions 0-3 only (--rc-regions), which
should keep the inner-region gains and return 120mm to the seed. Not yet run.

Members: 0.0389/0.0392/0.0386/0.0393/0.0391 = 0.0390 +- 0.0003 (five best members of the
project's 130+ configurations).
